The Problem

Classrooms are full of potential — but not all students benefit equally from live explanations.

Different learning speeds

Students absorb material at varying paces; one-size-fits-all pacing leaves many behind.

Lessons move too fast

When teachers progress quickly, knowledge gaps emerge that are hard to repair.

Limited review

Key classroom explanations are often ephemeral—students cannot always revisit them later.

Insufficient support

Teachers and students need tools that amplify learning without adding burdens.

How It Works

A simple, transparent flow that centers classroom teaching.

1

Teacher teaches

Lessons take place as normal. The system stays in the background and listens with teacher consent.

2

System captures lesson

Secure recording on the classroom device; minimal network usage and encrypted storage.

3

AI organizes knowledge

Transcription, topic segmentation, and tagging produce structured lesson records teachers can review.

4

Students revise and learn

Students access searchable lesson segments, practice prompts, and teacher-approved summaries.

Privacy & Ethics

We prioritize safety, consent, and fairness in every design decision.

Student privacy protection

All recordings are encrypted in transit and at rest; access is restricted to authorized users only.

Responsible access

Teachers control what is stored and shared. Administrators cannot access content without policies and consent.

Fair use of classroom data

Data is used only to support learning outcomes — not for profiling or commercial targeting.

Protecting teachers & students

Audit logs, opt-in capture, and teacher review workflows guard against misuse or misunderstandings.
